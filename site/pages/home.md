## Supporting Local Communities and Keeping the Tradition Alive

## Our handmade carpets are woven by local Nepalese Community. These Carpets are made of natural materials, have a double-knotted technique, and have a high pile density. Its main purpose is to keep local traditions and cultures alive in Nepal. We also ship our Carpets throughout the world.
