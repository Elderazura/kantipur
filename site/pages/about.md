## About Us
Kantipur Carpet Craft Industries is a mark of quality and an expert source of ideas for making your home come alive with true timeless beauty. It is our pleasure to take you on an extraordinary journey that spans the globe and shares with you a world of cultural mystique through exquisite hand-knotted area rugs. Though styles have come and gone, beneath them all is the fundamental principal that K.C.CI’s area rugs are created to touch your senses. From concept through production, a member of the K.C.C.I’s family is there making sure that the highest standards are being met. We offer a wide selection of Nepalese hand knotted area rugs in a variety of qualities.
Our rugs are made from the finest natural and synthetic fibres. It is ensuring quality and delivery; we maintain our own factories, where strong traditions of quality rug making have existed for generations. With over 15 years of commitment to excellence in quality and design, K.C.C.I continues to create a lasting impression in today’s marketplace. The vibrant; lively texture, of the distinctive appeal of K.C.C.I inevitably result from the hours of work dedication put in them. To us, “Carpet weaving” is a folk art that encompasses generations, heritage through which we express our age old traditions, religion and art. Our extensive inventory offers you the opportunity to make selections not only for smaller design projects, but also for your most lavish undertaking.
K.C.C.I carries over 500 carpet designs available in a range of sizes and colour. Special orders, involving custom colours and designs, are available in sizes as large as 25 feet by 40feet. Chang Carpet Manufacturers Industries is the sister concern of K.C.C.I which is specialized for American design. It is established with the underlining motto of production of traditional charm without compromising in the quality. Contemporary designs with a profusion of traditional details is one of the fortes of C.C.M.I. A sophisticated play of colour is done to match the various shades of your choice. A variety of textures can be achieved through vegetable dves, loop and cut piles , wool and silk mixtures ,pashmina and silk mixtures and antique weaves which are made by Nepalese weavers.
Within recent years the trend of the rug industry has been changing fast in relation to the continuous change of fashion in color, design, and quality of process. In order to keep up with this change and market demand, C.C.M.I keeps track with the best possible demand of designer colors, workmanship, and material quality. Presently, we have over 200 looms worked by almost 1200 adult Nepalese weavers who constantly weave the most magnificent contemporary and authentic designs recreated from classic antique rugs All of the handmade rugs you see in this catalogue are available in an assortment of sizes and colors. We have more designs available in our inventory. If you cannot find exactly what you are looking for or want more information on any of the rugs here, please contact us via phone, fax, or E-mail.
